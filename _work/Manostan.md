## [Give a visit ⎘](https://krittin-lee.github.io/Manostan/)

𓁹 Sonder is the realization that every person we encounter, even in the briefest passing glance, carries a life as complete and intricate as our own. Each has a story woven from hopes, fears, memories, and dreams, unfolding quietly within a world of their own.
<br><br>
Though we may differ in time, age, destination, or desire, the city brings our lives into an inevitable convergence. We inhabit the same surroundings, walk the same streets, wait at the same bus stops, and look upon the same buildings and city lights — on different days, in different seasons, or through different versions of ourselves.
<br><br>
A fleeting thought in the mind of a stranger, born in some particular place, may seem so small as to hold little significance. Yet it was once real, arising from a real life — shaped by decisions both great and small, by the people who crossed their path, and by moments accumulated over time. Slowly, these experiences settle into the words that form a single thought, in that particular moment.
<br><br>
The place before us — its light and shadows, the sound of passing cars, the warmth of the wind, the silence, or the restless hum of a crowd — is more than a backdrop. It is part of the subtle forces that stir certain thoughts into being, giving rise to the quiet voice within our minds. And so, each of us carries memories tethered to the same physical world, even as we experience it in ways entirely our own.
<br><br>
𓂀 Manosathan was conceived as a way to explore and reconstruct these invisible traces, and to offer a gentle reminder that, within any shared space, the people around us each inhabit an experience uniquely their own — one that can never be replaced by another, even when we walk the same streets of the same city.

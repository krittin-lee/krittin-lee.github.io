About

Krittin Leewanich is a student and creative technologist based in Bangkok, Thailand.

His work focuses on geographic and visual data systems, exploring how maps, images, and open technologies shape human understanding of space and shared environments. Whereas his work is often technical, he is also interested in the poetic and artistic aspects of computation, and how creative practices can inform the way people see and understand the world around them.

He is interested in the meeting point between computation and observation, especially how maps, open technologies, and visual languages can shape the way people see the world around them.

For four years, he had been a student at Chulalongkorn University where he studied computer science and design, and worked on projects related to geographic data, environmental monitoring, and experimental interfaces.
